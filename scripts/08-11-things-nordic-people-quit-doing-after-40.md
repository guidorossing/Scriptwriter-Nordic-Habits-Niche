Number ten. Apologising for going home.

Norwegian offices empty at four. Not gradually. They empty.

Norway did not solve that with willpower either. It solved it structurally. Number nine. The dinner party.

Number eight. Buying clothes for a life you do not live.

Open a Norwegian wardrobe at forty-five and it is genuinely boring. Wool. Wool. A shell jacket. Two pairs of boots, one for wet and one for cold. A dark jumper for the times you have to look like an adult.
